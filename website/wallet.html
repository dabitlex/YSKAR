YSKAR Wallet

Twelve words. Yours alone.

YSKAR Wallet is the Android app for YSR: send, receive, sign. Your key is created on your phone and stays there.

Download the APK Install in four steps

Version 2.0.0, for Android 7 or newer. Open source under the MIT License. English and German. The screen shows sample data.

Your balance, and nothing in the way.

No ads, no tracking and no account with anyone. The app talks to the YSKAR network and to nothing else.

Send on purpose

Enter an address, pick a contact or scan a QR code. Before sending you see recipient, amount and network fee once more, then you slide to confirm. A transfer cannot be taken back.

One history for everything.

Payments, block rewards and pool shares appear in one place. Amounts are exact to the 8th decimal place.

Receive and request

Show your QR code or your address. If you like, ask for a specific amount: the sender then only has to confirm.

Names instead of addresses.

Save a contact once, and every payment with that person sits in a single thread.

Locked down

PIN and fingerprint, automatic lock after a time you choose, no screenshots while the 12 words are on screen, and the app's own keyboard, so no input passes through your phone's keyboard app.

Whoever has the twelve words has the funds. Write them on paper. Nobody from YSKAR will ever ask you for them.

    Your key stays with you

    The wallet is created on your phone. 12 recovery words restore it on any device. They are stored encrypted with your PIN in Android's protected storage and never go into cloud backups.

    Sign and sign in

    Prove with a signed message that an address is yours, and check other people's signatures. Sign in to YSKAR Lab with a QR code. A signature cannot move YSR.

    Install in four steps

    The wallet is distributed as an APK through its GitHub releases. Updates install over the previous version and your wallet stays on the device.

    Download the APK

    1. Open the latest release on your Android phone and download the file yskar-wallet-…apk.

    2. Open the file. Allow installing from this source when Android asks.

    3. Tap “Create wallet” and write the 12 words down on paper, in order.

    4. Choose a PIN. To move an existing wallet over, enter its 12 words instead.

    Check that the file is genuine

    Every release is signed with the same key. This is the SHA-256 fingerprint of its certificate. If the fingerprint of your download differs, do not install the file.

    Where the wallet stands

    Android only

    The wallet is an APK from GitHub. It is not in an app store, and there is no iOS app.

    Ready for revision 6

    Version 2.0.0 knows the new transfer format and switches to it at block 10,000. An older version still receives YSR but cannot send after that block.

    Planned: an app store

    A release through Google Play is planned. There is no date.

    Planned: your own node

    Today the wallet reads from the project's main node. Connecting it to a node you run yourself is planned, so that nobody has to take the project's word for a balance.

    Other places for your YSR

    Every YSKAR app uses the same 12 words. With them you reach the same balance everywhere.

    YSKAR Wallet

    Android. Send, receive, sign. It does not mine.

    Node Core

    Windows and Linux. A wallet inside your own full node, with mining and message signing. Get Node Core

    Android app with mining

    Wallet and mining on the phone, with a home-screen widget and notifications.

    Telegram Mini App

    The easy way in, with nothing to install. Ownership depends on your 12 words, not on your Telegram account.
