YSKAR is a currency with a blockchain of its own. Nothing was handed out in advance: every YSR is mined, and anyone can check every block. Fair from block 0.

It did not start with a promise. It started with a block.

Nothing was mined in advance. Nothing was handed out. About 1.07 million YSR from the early days were burned to offset the head start.

Since then a new YSR comes into the world in one way only: a machine does the work, and anyone can check it.

Don't take our word for it.

Every block carries its own proof. Your device can check it right now: no server, no account, only arithmetic.

Hash of the block header

What your device just did: it ran the 136 bytes of the block header through SHA-256 twice. The result has to be smaller than the target the network set for that block. The zeros at the start show how small it is.
