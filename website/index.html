In short

What is YSKAR?

YSKAR is a cryptocurrency with a blockchain of its own. Its coin is called YSR.

New YSR come into existence in one way only: mining. The participants’ devices compete to find the next block, and whoever finds it receives the reward. Nothing was allocated in advance, and there will never be more than 21 million YSR.

Anyone can take part with what they have: a phone, a PC or a graphics card. Your wallet lives on your own device; nobody else holds your YSR.

YSKAR is a community project and still young. The network has been running since 9 September 2026, currently on very few nodes. Its present limits are named openly in the whitepaper.

Step by step

How YSKAR works

From the first hash to the YSR in your wallet, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Devices compute

    Phones, PCs and graphics cards search at the same time for a number that makes the next block valid. That is mining: pure computation with SHA-256d.

  2. 02

    A block is found

    The network sets the difficulty so that a block is found about every 10 minutes. The new block contains the waiting transfers and attaches itself to the previous one.

  3. 03

    The reward is paid

    The block itself pays the reward, currently 875 YSR: to the finder alone, or in a pool to everyone by the work they contributed.

  4. 04

    Everyone verifies

    Every node re-computes every block completely: the work, the signatures, the balances. A block that breaks a rule is rejected, no matter who sent it.

Every block points to the one before it. Anyone who wanted to change an old block would have to redo the work of all blocks after it.

Your wallet

12 words create your key on your own device. You sign transfers with it; without it nobody can move your YSR.

Sending and receiving

A transfer is sent to the network and written into the chain with one of the next blocks. With every further block it becomes more secure.

Solo or in a pool

Solo, the finder receives the whole reward. In a pool the block splits it directly among everyone; the pool never holds anyone else’s money.

Run your own node

A node checks every block itself, starting from block 0. The more nodes there are, the less YSKAR depends on any single computer. A Raspberry Pi is enough.

  • Node.js 22.13 or newer
  • Runs on Linux, Windows and macOS
  • Can run its own pool, no sign-up needed

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to take part?

No. The app, Mini App, Node Core and miner are free. You pay for the electricity your device uses; sending YSR costs a small network fee, and a pool keeps its fee (the public pool: 1 %).

What if I lose my phone?

With your 12 words you can restore your wallet on any device. Without them, nobody can recover the funds, not even us.

Why doesn’t the Play Store app mine?

Google Play does not allow apps that mine cryptocurrency on the device itself. That is why the Android app with mining is available directly on this website.

Is the code open source?

Yes. The entire code – node, app, miner and this website – is public on GitHub and released under the MIT License.

How do I know the numbers are real?

The explorer checks the proof of work of every block in your browser and follows the chain. A full node or the observer goes further and verifies every transfer, signature and balance from block 0 – anyone can run one.
