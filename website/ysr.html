21 million. Every one of them mined.

YSR is the coin of the YSKAR chain. There will never be more than 21,000,000. Nothing was pre-mined or allocated: a new YSR exists for one reason only, a block was found.

21,000,000. Never more.

Each light stands for YSR, in the order they come into being. This is everything there will ever be.

That many exist today, of the total. Every one of them came out of a block that somebody found.

The head start was burned.

About 1.07 million YSR from the early days were burned to offset the head start. They went to an address nobody can spend from and are gone for good.

See it in block 3,974

Half of everything comes first.

The first 12,000 blocks create 10.5 million YSR: half of all there will ever be. The chain is at block .

Then it halves, again and again.

Every 12,000 blocks the reward is cut in half. The last new YSR appears in block 443,999. After that, miners earn fees only.

Fees

A transfer pays a fee to the miners of the block that includes it. The rules set only a floor. Above it, the price follows the demand for space in a block.

No price, no promise

YSR has no price and no market, and it is not listed on any exchange. Nothing on this site promises that this will change, and nobody should mine in that expectation.

What YSR has is a set of rules that anyone can check: a fixed maximum, a public issuance schedule, and coins that only their owner's key can move.
